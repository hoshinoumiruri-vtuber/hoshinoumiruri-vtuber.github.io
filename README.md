## 中文

星ノ海琉璃的直播、唱過的歌、精選時刻和角色設定的整理網站：https://hoshinoumiruri-vtuber.github.io

- 每一場直播、每一首歌都有自己的網頁，點時間或播放鍵會用內嵌的 YouTube 播放器，從琉璃頻道原直播的那個時間點開始播放。
- 這個 repo 是網站本身，內容由程式產生，請不要直接修改這裡的檔案。這裡沒有任何影音檔，影片都是內嵌 YouTube。
- 內容來自對直播存檔的整理：歌曲時間點由聲音比對和歌單整理得出，精選時刻先由程式找出，再逐筆篩選。
- 標示「粉絲頻道」的單曲影片在粉絲頻道「琉星播放器」，不是琉璃本人頻道的影片。
- 琉璃頻道沒有存檔的早期直播，播放的是備份頻道「琉星芭樂騎士直播補檔」的影片，頁面上會標示「備份頻道」。
- 網站有繁體中文和日文兩種介面（日文在 `/ja/`），直播標題和歌名維持原文。
- 角色設定頁的三視圖：Design TENO、Live2D Crim、Logo Design akiguri。圖片的著作權屬於星ノ海琉璃和各位繪師，請不要轉載、加工或用於 AI 學習。
- 資料有錯或希望移除某些內容，請聯絡琉璃：https://lit.link/zh-tw/HoshinoumiRuri

## English

A site that organises the streams, songs, highlights and character sheet of Hoshinoumi Ruri (星ノ海琉璃), a Taiwanese VTuber: https://hoshinoumiruri-vtuber.github.io

- Every stream and every song has a page of its own. Timestamps and play buttons open an embedded YouTube player at that moment of the archived stream on Ruri's own channel.
- This repository is the website itself. Its files are generated by a program, so please do not edit them by hand. There are no video or audio files here, every video is an embedded YouTube player.
- The content comes from going through the stream archives: song times come from matching audio and from song lists, and the highlights are found by a program and then screened one by one.
- Clips marked "fan channel" are on the fan channel 琉星播放器 and are not videos of Ruri's own channel.
- Early streams that are no longer on Ruri's channel play from the backup channel 琉星芭樂騎士直播補檔, and the page says so.
- The interface is in Traditional Chinese and in Japanese (Japanese is under `/ja/`). Titles of streams and songs are shown as they are.
- Character sheet: design by TENO, Live2D by Crim, logo by akiguri. The copyright of the pictures belongs to Hoshinoumi Ruri and the artists. Please do not repost, edit, or use them to train AI.
- If something is wrong, or you would like something removed, please contact Ruri: https://lit.link/zh-tw/HoshinoumiRuri

- 内容は配信アーカイブを整理したものです。曲の時刻は音声の照合とセトリの整理で求め、名場面はプログラムが見つけたものを一件ずつ確認して絞っています。
